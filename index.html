How are we working with AI?

We're putting together a plan for how KOKO uses AI — and we want it to be shaped by the people who actually do the work.

This survey is completely anonymous. No names, no emails, nothing that could identify you. We only ask for your department so we can spot patterns across teams and make sure nobody gets left behind.

There are no right or wrong answers. Be as honest as you can — that's the only thing that makes this useful.

On anonymity: Individual responses will never be shared. Results are only reported at department level, and only where a group is large enough that no individual could be identified. We'll share the findings back with the whole company and use them to shape how we move forward together.
